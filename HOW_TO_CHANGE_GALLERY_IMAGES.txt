HOW TO ADD GALLERY IMAGES

1. Copy your new photo into the images folder.
   Example: images/new-room-photo.webp

2. Open gallery.html.

3. Search for: GALLERY IMAGE CONTROL

4. Add one new line inside GALLERY_IMAGES:
   { src: "images/new-room-photo.webp", alt: "Description of the photo for SEO" },

5. Save the file.

Notes:
- Do not edit the gallery HTML layout.
- The gallery is created automatically from the GALLERY_IMAGES list.
- Images are shown in full with their natural aspect ratio, not cropped.
