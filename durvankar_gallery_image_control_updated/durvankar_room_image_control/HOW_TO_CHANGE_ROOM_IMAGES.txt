ROOM IMAGE CHANGE GUIDE

Open index.html and search: ROOM IMAGE CONTROL

Change only these 4 lines:
--room-deluxe-image: url('images/deluxe-ac-room.webp');
--room-family-image: url('images/family-room.webp');
--room-standard-image: url('images/standard-room.webp');
--room-bungalow-image: url('images/durvankar-property.webp');

Keep your new image inside the images folder and update only the filename.
No UI/CSS/layout changes are needed.
